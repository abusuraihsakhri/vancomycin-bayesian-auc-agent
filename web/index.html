Vancomycin PK exposure explorer

One-compartment steady-state exposure model, with an optional penalized population-parameter fit to one serum concentration.

For research and education only. Not a clinical dosing calculator.

Not validated for patient care, therapeutic drug monitoring, or individualized dose selection. Do not enter patient identifiers. Model assumes fixed intermittent IV infusions, steady state, stable renal function and a simplified population prior. In particular, a single level cannot uniquely identify clearance and distribution volume.
